Planning the trip

When to go

May–June and September–October avoid the August crowds and heat, while still giving warm, reliable weather across most regions.

Getting around

High-speed rail connects most major cities in under four hours; a car is the better choice for the hill towns and coastlines off the main lines.

Off the main circuit

Basilicata, Molise and inland Sicily offer the Italy locals still recognise — far fewer crowds than Rome, Florence or Venice.
