Regional cooking at a glance

The North

Butter, rice and cream country — risotto alla milanese, Bolognese ragù, and the Alpine cheeses and cured meats of Trentino and Piedmont.

The Centre

Olive oil, handmade pasta and simple, ingredient-led cooking — Tuscan bistecca, Roman carbonara, Umbrian truffles.

The South & Islands

Tomatoes, seafood and chilli heat — Neapolitan pizza, Calabrian 'nduja, Sicilian street food and Sardinian pecorino.
