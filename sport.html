The big three

Calcio (football)

Serie A's rivalries — Milan vs Inter, Roma vs Lazio, Juventus's dominance — run deeper than most leagues in Europe, and four World Cup stars prove it.

Giro d'Italia

May — three weeks of road racing through the Alps, the Dolomites and the full length of the peninsula, Italy's answer to the Tour de France.

Formula 1 at Monza

September — the 'Temple of Speed' and Ferrari's home race, one of the oldest and fastest circuits still on the calendar.
