What carries abroad

Language & dialect

Standard Italian is barely 150 years old as a spoken-by-everyone language — most families abroad carry a regional dialect alongside it, from Napulitano to Sicilianu to Veneto.

Art & architecture

From Giotto to Michelangelo to Renzo Piano, Italy's visual language has shaped how the rest of the world builds and paints.

Cinema & opera

Neorealism, Fellini, Sergio Leone's westerns, and an opera tradition running from Verdi to La Scala's stage today.

Culture kept alive a generation on

Italian cultural societies, dialect-speaking grandparents, and parish feast-day processions have kept regional identity alive abroad for well over a century — often more faithfully preserved overseas than back home.
