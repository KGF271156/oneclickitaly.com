Throughout the year

National dates worth knowing

Carnevale

February — Venice's masked carnival is the most famous, but towns across every region hold their own version.

Easter & Holy Week

Processions, re-enactments and regional specialities mark Italy's biggest religious calendar moment.

Ferragosto

15 August — the nationwide August bank holiday when most of Italy closes down and heads to the coast or the mountains.

Christmas markets

December — from Bolzano's Alpine markets to Naples' presepe (nativity scene) workshops on Via San Gregorio Armeno.
