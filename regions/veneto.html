What makes Veneto, Veneto

Venice

A city built on water, unlike anywhere else on Earth — canals instead of streets, gondolas instead of cars, and over a thousand years of maritime history.

Prosecco hills

The UNESCO-listed hills of Valdobbiadene and Conegliano, where steep terraced vineyards produce Italy's most exported sparkling wine.

Verona & Palladio

Romeo and Juliet's city, plus the Palladian villas scattered across the Veneto countryside that shaped Western architecture for centuries.

Events

Worth planning around

Carnevale di Venezia

February — one of the world's most famous carnivals, masks and all.

Festa del Redentore

Third Sunday of July — a floating bridge of boats and fireworks over the Giudecca Canal.

Verona Opera Festival

Summer — opera staged in the 2,000-year-old Roman Arena di Verona.

Venice Film Festival

August/September — the world's oldest film festival, held on the Lido.

Venetian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Venetian and broader Veneto emigration shaped entire regions abroad — notably Brazil's Rio Grande do Sul and Argentina's wine country — carrying viticulture and masonry trades that still define those communities today.
