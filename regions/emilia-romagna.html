What makes Emilia-Romagna, Emilia-Romagna

Bologna, 'la dotta, la grassa'

'The learned, the fat' — home to Europe's oldest university and a food culture that gave the world ragù, tortellini and mortadella.

Motor Valley

Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati and Ducati all build within a short drive of Modena — a concentration of automotive engineering with no equal.

Ravenna's mosaics

Byzantine mosaics so well preserved that UNESCO lists eight separate Ravenna monuments as World Heritage sites.
