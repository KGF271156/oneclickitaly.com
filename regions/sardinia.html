Sardinia — an island apart.

Mediterranean Italy's largest island, with its own language, its own ancient nuraghi towers, and a coastline that rivals the Caribbean for colour.

What makes Sardinia, Sardinia

The nuraghi

Over 7,000 Bronze Age stone towers dot the island, built by a civilisation that predates Rome and still isn't fully understood.

Costa Smeralda

The 'Emerald Coast' of the northeast — turquoise coves that have drawn the international jet set since the 1960s.

A language of its own

Sardinian (Sardu) is recognised as the most distinct Romance language from Latin, still spoken alongside Italian across the island.

Events

Worth planning around

Sant'Efisio Festival

1 May — a four-day procession and one of Italy's oldest and largest religious festivals.

Cavalcata Sarda

May — Sassari's grand parade of traditional costumes from across the island.

Redentore di Nuoro

August — a major folk-costume festival in the island's mountainous heart.

Autunno in Barbagia

Autumn — weekly open-house festivals through the Barbagia region's ancient hill villages.
