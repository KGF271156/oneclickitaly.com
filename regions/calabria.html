What makes Calabria, Calabria

Tropea & the coast

Clifftop Tropea, famous for its sweet red onions, overlooks some of the clearest water in Italy along the Costa degli Dei.

Aspromonte & Sila

Two rugged mountain ranges of dense pine forest and Byzantine-era hill villages, a world away from the coast below.

A Byzantine & Greek past

Calabria's deep south still holds Griko-speaking villages, a living trace of Magna Graecia that predates Rome itself.

Events

Worth planning around

Festival del Peperoncino

September — Diamante's chilli pepper festival, celebrating the region's famously fiery cooking.

Palio dei Quartieri

Summer — neighbourhood processions and historic re-enactments across coastal towns.

Festa della Madonna di Polsi

September — a major pilgrimage deep into the Aspromonte mountains.

Peperoncino harvest season

Late summer — when roadside stalls across the region overflow with the year's chilli crop.

Calabrian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Calabrian communities built some of the largest Italian-diaspora footholds abroad, from Melbourne to Toronto to New York, with regional clubs (circoli) still anchoring community life generations on.
