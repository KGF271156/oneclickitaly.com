What makes Sicily, Sicily

Mount Etna

Europe's tallest and most active volcano, looming over vineyards that grow in its own volcanic soil.

The Valley of the Temples

Agrigento's astonishingly preserved Greek temples, among the best-surviving examples of Greek architecture anywhere — including Greece itself.

Arab-Norman Palermo

A capital city layered with Byzantine mosaics, Arab geometry and Norman stonework, found nowhere else in quite this combination.

Events

Worth planning around

Infiorata di Noto

May — baroque Noto's streets carpeted in elaborate flower-petal murals.

Festa di Santa Rosalia

July — Palermo's enormous patron-saint festival, 'u fistinu', filling the city for days.

Taormina Film Fest

Summer — an international film festival staged in a 3rd-century BC Greek theatre.

Carnevale di Acireale

February — widely considered Sicily's most spectacular carnival, famed for its flower floats.

Sicilian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

No region shaped the global image of Italian emigration more than Sicily — from New York to Buenos Aires to Melbourne, Sicilian communities remain among the largest and most visible of the Italian diaspora worldwide.
