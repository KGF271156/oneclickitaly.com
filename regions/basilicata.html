What makes Basilicata, Basilicata

The Sassi of Matera

Ancient cave-dwellings carved directly into limestone cliffs, inhabited for millennia and now a UNESCO World Heritage city and former European Capital of Culture.

Pollino National Park

Italy's largest national park, straddling Basilicata and Calabria — wild, mountainous, and still home to the odd wolf.

A region overlooked, on purpose

Basilicata's isolation kept it poor for centuries, but preserved a rawness and authenticity now drawing travellers tired of the usual circuit.

Worth planning around

Maggio di Accettura

May — an ancient tree-marriage ritual rooted in pre-Christian fertility rites.

Volo dell'Angelo

Summer — a cable-zipline 'flight' between the clifftop towns of Castelmezzano and Pietrapertosa.

Festa della Bruna

2 July — Matera's spectacular saint's-day festival, ending with a triumphal cart being torn apart by the crowd.

Matera Film Festival

Autumn — celebrating the Sassi's growing reputation as a filming location.
