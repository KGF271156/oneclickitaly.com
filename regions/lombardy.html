What makes Lombardy, Lombardy

Milan, fashion & finance

Italy's business capital and a global fashion-week fixture, anchored by the vast Gothic Duomo and Leonardo's Last Supper.

The Lakes

Como, Garda and Maggiore — glamorous villas, mountain backdrops and a slower pace just an hour from Milan's traffic.

A region of contrasts

From the Alpine peaks of Valtellina to the flat rice paddies of the Po Valley, Lombardy packs an outsized geographic range.

Lombard abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Lombard emigration, especially from Milan and Bergamo, carried strong craft and industrial trades abroad, shaping manufacturing communities from the US Rust Belt to São Paulo.
