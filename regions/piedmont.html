What makes Piedmont, Piedmont

Turin, Italy's first capital

Elegant, grid-planned and underrated, Turin was unified Italy's first capital and remains home to the Shroud of Turin and the FIAT industrial legacy.

Barolo & the Langhe

Rolling UNESCO-listed vineyard hills producing some of Italy's most celebrated wines, alongside the prized white truffles of Alba.

The Italian Alps

Piedmont shares the Alps with France and Switzerland, including the 1956 and 2006 Winter Olympics host region around Sestriere.
