What makes Trentino-South Tyrol, Trentino-South Tyrol

The Dolomites

Jagged, pink-tinged limestone peaks that turn the sky fire-orange at sunset (the 'enrosadira') — a UNESCO World Heritage landscape.

A region of two cultures

South Tyrol (Südtirol) is majority German-speaking after centuries under Habsburg rule, while Trentino to the south remains firmly Italian — both proudly autonomous.

Ötzi the Iceman

A 5,300-year-old mummy found frozen in a glacier here is now the star exhibit of Bolzano's archaeology museum.

Dolomites Hike & Bike

Trentino-Tyrolean abroad

The diaspora carries it on

South Tyrolean and Trentine emigration carried a distinct Alpine identity abroad — notably to Brazil's Rio Grande do Sul, where entire towns still speak a preserved dialect of Trentino's own.
