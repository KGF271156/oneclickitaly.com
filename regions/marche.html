Marche — Italy's best-kept secret.

Rolling hills, a quiet Adriatic coast and Raphael's birthplace, Urbino — a region Italians love precisely because tourists mostly skip it.

What makes Marche, Marche

Urbino's Renaissance legacy

The birthplace of Raphael and a perfectly preserved Renaissance hill town, UNESCO-listed almost in its entirety.

Monte Conero

A dramatic limestone headland rising straight from the Adriatic — the region's one true mountain-meets-sea moment.

A region of small towns

No single dominant city, just dozens of fortified hill towns each with their own festivals, food and dialect quirks.

Events

Worth planning around

Rossini Opera Festival

August — Pesaro's festival honouring its most famous son, composer Gioachino Rossini.

Palio di Fermo

Summer — a historic costumed horse race and procession through Fermo's streets.

Infiorate di Pinturicchio

June — flower-petal street art in villages across the region.

Sferisterio Opera Festival

Summer — Macerata's open-air opera season in a 19th-century arena.

Marchigiano abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Marchigiano emigration, though smaller in number than the south's, built strong artisan and agricultural communities abroad, particularly in the northeastern United States.
