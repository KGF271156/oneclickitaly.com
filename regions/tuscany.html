What makes Tuscany, Tuscany

Florence & the Renaissance

The Duomo, the Uffizi, Ponte Vecchio — the city that produced Dante, Michelangelo and Machiavelli, and still feels lived-in rather than roped-off.

Siena & the Palio

Twice a year the Piazza del Campo becomes a bareback horse race between city districts — centuries-old rivalry, run at full tilt.

Chianti & the countryside

Cypress-lined roads, hilltop towns like San Gimignano and Montepulciano, and the vineyards that gave the world Chianti and Brunello.

Tuscany abroad

The diaspora carries it on

From the Tuscan vineyards that seeded California's wine country to the network of Tuscan-American and Tuscan-Australian clubs keeping dialect and dish alive a generation on, Tuscany's reach abroad runs deep.
