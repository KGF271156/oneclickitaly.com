What makes Umbria, Umbria

Assisi

St Francis's hometown, its vast basilica frescoed by Giotto, draws pilgrims and art lovers in equal measure to this hillside town.

Perugia's chocolate legacy

Home to Perugina and the Baci kiss-shaped chocolate, plus Eurochocolate, one of Europe's biggest chocolate festivals every October.

The green heart of Italy

Umbria's official nickname — rolling, forested and famously the only Italian region with neither a coastline nor a border with another country.

Umbria Jazz

July — one of Europe's premier jazz festivals, filling Perugia for ten days.

Eurochocolate

October — Perugia transforms into a city-wide chocolate festival.

Calendimaggio di Assisi

May — a medieval-costumed competition between Assisi's upper and lower districts.

Festa dei Ceri

15 May — Gubbio's breakneck race carrying towering wooden 'candles' through the streets.

Umbrian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Umbrian emigration, smaller in scale than its southern neighbours, nonetheless built close-knit regional associations abroad that still gather around Assisi's feast days and Umbrian cooking.
