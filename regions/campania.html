What makes Campania, Campania

Naples, loud and alive

Chaotic, dense and endlessly vivid — the birthplace of pizza, opera's Teatro di San Carlo, and a street life unlike anywhere else in Italy.

Pompeii & Herculaneum

Entire Roman cities frozen by Vesuvius in 79 AD, still being excavated nearly 2,000 years later.

The Amalfi Coast & Capri

Switchback cliff roads, lemon groves and pastel towns stacked above the Tyrrhenian Sea — among the most photographed coastlines on Earth.

Events

Worth planning around

Festa di San Gennaro

19 September — Naples' patron saint festival, centred on the famous 'liquefying blood' ritual.

Ravello Festival

Summer — classical concerts staged on clifftop terraces above the Amalfi Coast.

Pompeii by night

Summer evenings — the ruins open late, far cooler and quieter than the daytime crowds.

Capri Hollywood

December/January — an international film festival on the island of Capri.

Campanian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

No Italian region shaped the diaspora's image abroad quite like Campania — Little Italy neighbourhoods from New York to Buenos Aires were built largely by Neapolitan hands, and the food culture that followed changed how the world eats Italian.
