Liguria — the Italian Riviera.

A narrow crescent of coastline squeezed between mountains and sea, home to Cinque Terre's cliffside villages and the birthplace of pesto.

What makes Liguria, Liguria

Cinque Terre

Five pastel-coloured villages clinging to cliffs above the Ligurian Sea, connected by footpaths carved into the rock since medieval times.

Genoa, the Superba

Once one of the great maritime republics, Genoa's old port and labyrinthine caruggi (alleys) still hint at its seafaring wealth.

A coastline of terraces

Centuries of hand-built stone terraces climb the hills behind the coast, still used to grow the basil that makes Ligurian pesto famous.

Events

Worth planning around

Palio Marinaro del Tigullio

Summer — historic rowing races between Ligurian coastal towns.

Sagra del Pesto

Spring/summer — Genoa's celebration of its most famous export, pesto.

Euroflora

Held periodically — one of Europe's great flower shows, staged in Genoa.

Festa di San Giovanni

24 June — bonfires and processions along the Riviera marking the feast of St John.

Ligurian abroad

The diaspora carries it on

Genoa's centuries as a maritime power sent Ligurians abroad earlier than almost any other region — notably to South America, where Genoese communities shaped early Argentine and Peruvian immigration.
