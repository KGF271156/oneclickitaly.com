What makes Lazio, Lazio

Rome, layer upon layer

Ancient, Renaissance, Baroque and modern Rome sit stacked on top of each other — the Colosseum, the Pantheon and the Vatican all within walking distance.

Tivoli's villas

Villa d'Este's cascading fountains and Hadrian's sprawling imperial retreat, both UNESCO-listed day trips from the capital.

Beyond the capital

Lazio's coastline, the volcanic Lake Bracciano, and Etruscan necropolises give the region a quieter side most visitors to Rome never see.
